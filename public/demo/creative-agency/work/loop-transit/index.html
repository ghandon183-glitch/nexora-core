Making public transit maps people actually enjoy reading

Loop Transit Co-op

The challenge

Loop's routes covered a growing city, but their maps hadn't kept up — riders regularly got lost transferring between the bike-share and bus network.

Our approach

We redesigned the entire wayfinding system around a single color-coded logic that carries through printed signage, the website's live map, and the mobile app, tested with actual riders at three stations before rollout.

The outcome

Support tickets tagged "couldn't find my stop" dropped by 58% in the two months following rollout.
