Our Philosophy

We don't list houses. We represent buildings.

Every home we represent has been chosen by hand — for the architect who designed it, the materials it was built from, or the story it has quietly carried for decades.

We work with a small number of clients at a time, because a building like this deserves more than a listing photo and a lockbox.
